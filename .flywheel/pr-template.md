## Scenario

Who, by role, does what. A change that nobody meets in use, such as a refactor or a CI fix, names who would notice it and why it is made.

### Before

What happened before the change. Where it covers more than one behavior, each behavior is a bullet.

### After

What happens after it. Where it covers more than one behavior, each is a bullet, in the Before's order.

## Change

What the pull request changes. Where it departs from the issue, how and why, in a sentence or two and in terms the description has already defined. For what the issue proposed and the pull request leaves as it is, a pointer to the issue rather than an account of it.
